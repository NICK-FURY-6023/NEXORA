# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in NEXORA to be a good experience for everyone, in the
repository, in issues, in pull requests and in any space the project runs —
issue trackers, chats, voice channels, or elsewhere.

We pledge to act in ways that make this project open to people of all
backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. People disagree about design; argue about the design,
  not about the person.
- Be respectful. No harassment, no personal attacks, no insulting or
  derogatory comments.
- Keep it professional. No sexualised language or imagery, no racism, no
  sexism, no hate speech, no publishing someone's private information.
- Accept constructive critique gracefully. When your work is reviewed, the
  review is about the code, and "thanks, good catch" costs nothing.
- Take responsibility: apologise when you get something wrong, and help repair
  the damage.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences. A Rust audio server attracts
  people from many backgrounds; not everyone arrives knowing Lavalink's
  protocol.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments, including about someone's background.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery in project spaces.
- Deliberate misinformation about the project's behaviour, its security
  properties, or its compatibility — the compatibility matrix exists to be
  honest, and keeping it honest is a shared responsibility.
- Continued one-on-one contact after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer: **partham302@gmail.com**

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will review the report,
decide on a response, and — where the behaviour continues — act. You will not
be asked to relitigate the report in public.

You can also report behaviour through a GitHub issue **only** when the
behaviour is not a security matter and you are comfortable with it being
public; for anything security-related, use the private route in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers enforce this code of conduct. On a report, they will:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was wrong and why.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and a
   period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — from all project spaces, for a stated period.
4. **Permanent ban** — from all project spaces, for behaviour that is
   sustained, targeted or severe.

They will try to resolve a matter privately where that is possible and
proportionate. A ban is not a punishment to be relitigated; it is a decision
that the behaviour was not welcome.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, the
issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any voice or chat channel the
project runs — and to anyone representing the project publicly.
